About the event

Glen Striders Running Club was built on early mornings, shared kilometres and people who show up for each other. The Glen Striders 10K is how we open that circle wider.

The route will take you through landmarks, neighbourhoods or green spaces, with water points, marshals and club members cheering along the way.

A 5 km option will include junior prize categories, so children of different ages can race in their own age groups.

What to expect

  • A well-marshalled route
  • Water points along the course
  • Finisher medals for everyone
  • Overall and age-category prizes
  • Post-race refreshments and a club meet-up
  • A low-waste race: refillable water stations and a post-race litter pick
